Recap

What we heard

We had a great conversation last week working through the homework, and it informed our exploration. Thanks! Here's what stuck with us:

→The snail nods to an early "Slow Series" idea, a symbol of stillness and presence, not slowness.
→Confidence, not performance. Obama, Seth Rogen, and Amy Poehler all share comfort in their own skin. Nothing forced.
→Boldness through the work, not the claim. It shouldn't read as bragging, it's confidence.
→Timeless over trendy. Nostalgia is welcome, but nothing dated or chasing a moment.
→Warm, muted, tactile color language. Teal (not cyan), soft grain, human texture over anything oversaturated or digital feeling.
→Serious craft, light touch. Quality, professional work that's never stiff or overly formal.
